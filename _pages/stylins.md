Stylins.ai was a startup project I worked on that aimed to reimagine the online shopping experience through virtual try-on technology. The goal was to apply advanced Computer Vision methods to make it easier for people to visualize how clothes might look on them before making a purchase.  

The project focused on enabling effortless style exploration, helping users mix and match outfits virtually, and encouraging more confident purchase decisions. Beyond convenience, we also hoped that this technology could promote more sustainable fashion practices by reducing unnecessary returns and lowering the environmental impact of online shopping.  

While Stylins.ai is no longer active, the vision behind it reflected a commitment to combining innovation with responsible fashion choices—an attempt to bring together accuracy, personalization, and sustainability in the digital retail space.
